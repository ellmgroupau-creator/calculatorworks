Understanding Budgeting

What is budgeting?

Budgeting is the process of comparing income, expenses, savings goals, and future obligations to understand cash flow.

Common budget categories

The exact categories differ between households, countries, and personal circumstances.

  • Housing
  • Transport
  • Food
  • Utilities
  • Insurance
  • Debt repayments
  • Savings
  • Emergency funds

Why budgeting matters

A budget can help identify spending patterns, estimate affordability, and plan for future costs.

Use estimates carefully

Calculator results should be treated as planning estimates, not guarantees.
