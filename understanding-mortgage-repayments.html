Understanding Mortgage Repayments

Mortgage repayments are influenced by loan size, interest rates, repayment frequency, and loan term length.

Key Mortgage Factors

  • Interest rates
  • Fixed vs variable loans
  • Loan term length
  • Extra repayments
  • Offset accounts
  • Refinancing

Why Interest Matters

Even small changes in mortgage interest rates can significantly affect total repayment costs over decades.

Loan Affordability

Mortgage calculators can help borrowers estimate repayment affordability before purchasing property.
