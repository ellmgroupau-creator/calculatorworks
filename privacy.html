Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains the general privacy approach for CalculatorWorks visitors.

Information handled by the site

CalculatorWorks is a static calculator website. Calculator inputs are generally processed in the browser so users can receive quick estimates without submitting calculation values to a server-side calculator database.

The site may use standard hosting, security, analytics, consent, or advertising technologies that process limited technical information such as browser type, pages visited, device information, and consent choices.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Cookies or similar technologies may be used for essential functionality, consent preferences, analytics, or advertising where enabled. Users can manage cookies through browser settings and any consent tools shown on the site.

Contact and corrections

If you contact CalculatorWorks about a correction or website issue, information you provide may be used to review and respond to that request.

Use of the site is also subject to the terms and disclaimer.
