Editorial Policy

CalculatorWorks editorial standards, methodology principles, and educational content policy.

Editorial purpose

CalculatorWorks publishes calculators and educational resources intended for general informational and educational use. Pages are designed to help visitors complete a calculation, understand the formula, and review related tools or guidance.

The site avoids presenting calculator estimates as personalised advice. Finance, health, tax, legal, construction, and other high-impact topics are caveated so visitors understand that results should be verified before important decisions.

Editorial principles

CalculatorWorks prioritises clear usability, plain-language explanations, practical examples, fast-loading pages, mobile-friendly layout, transparent assumptions, and links to related calculators where they help visitors compare scenarios.

Content is intended to be generally useful across countries where possible. When a calculator depends heavily on local rules, the page should make clear that official sources or qualified professionals may be needed.

Review and correction approach

Maintenance work focuses on calculator integrity, broken-link repair, structural consistency, formula clarity, and user-first navigation. Feedback about unclear wording, calculation issues, or broken pages may be reviewed and used to improve the site.
