Site Quality Guidelines

CalculatorWorks aims to provide consistent, fast-loading, educational calculators and supporting resources.

Quality Principles

  • Clear navigation
  • Mobile-friendly layouts
  • Fast page loading
  • Consistent calculator structure
  • General educational content
  • Internationally applicable explanations where possible

Educational Purpose

CalculatorWorks calculators and guides are intended for general informational and educational purposes only.

Users should independently verify important calculations and seek qualified professional advice where appropriate.
