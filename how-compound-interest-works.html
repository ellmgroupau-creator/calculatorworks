How Compound Interest Works

Compound interest is one of the most important concepts in finance and long-term investing.

Unlike simple interest, compound interest allows earnings to grow on both the original principal and previously accumulated interest.

Compound Interest Formula

A = P(1 + r/n)nt

Why Compounding Matters

  • Long-term investment growth
  • Retirement planning
  • Savings accounts
  • Investment portfolios
  • Wealth accumulation

Example

If $10,000 earns 7% annually over 30 years, compounding may significantly increase the ending balance compared to simple interest.

Time Horizon Importance

The longer investments remain invested, the more powerful compounding becomes.
