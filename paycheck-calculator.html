Paycheck Calculator

Estimate gross and post-tax pay per pay period from annual salary, pay periods, and a flat tax rate.

Calculator inputs

Estimate gross and post-tax pay per pay period from annual salary, pay periods, and a flat tax rate.

Enter the amount in the currency you are using. Keep the same currency across money fields.

Number of pay periods in a full year. Common examples are 12 monthly, 24 semi-monthly, 26 biweekly, or 52 weekly.

Enter the estimated flat tax or deduction percentage for this scenario. For example, enter 24 for an estimated 24% rate.

Result
Enter a value to begin

Formula

Gross per period = salary ÷ pay periods; post-tax estimate = gross × (1 - tax rate).

$84,000 paid across 26 periods gives $3,230.77 gross and about $2,455.38 after 24% tax.

How to use paycheck results

Use the paycheck calculator to estimate take-home pay from gross pay and deductions. It is most useful when comparing pay frequencies or checking how a change in hours or salary may affect cash flow.

Before relying on the result: Payroll outcomes can vary by country, state, tax rules, benefits, deductions, overtime, and employer payroll settings.
