How this calculator works

This calculator divides project area by paver coverage and adds waste.

Use this result as a planning estimate. Real lending, payroll, tax, health or construction outcomes can vary based on official rules, local costs and personal circumstances.

Formula

Pavers = project area ÷ paver area × waste factor

Worked example

A 30 m² project using 0.04 m² pavers needs about 825 pavers with 10% waste.
