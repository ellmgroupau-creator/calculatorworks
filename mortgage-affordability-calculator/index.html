A payment-based borrowing estimate

This calculator works backwards from a monthly principal-and-interest budget. It does not assess income, living expenses, credit history or lender serviceability rules. The result is a loan-size scenario, not approval or a safe spending limit.

Formula

P = M × (1 − (1 + r)−n) ÷ r

M is the monthly payment budget, r = annual rate ÷ 100 ÷ 12 and n = years × 12. At 0%, P = M × n. Estimated purchase price = loan size + entered deposit. Purchase costs must be allowed for separately.

Worked example

A $2,800 monthly payment budget at 6.5% over 30 years supports approximately $442,990 of borrowing. Adding an $80,000 deposit gives a purchase-price scenario of about $522,990, before transaction costs. A higher rate reduces the supported loan size.

Budget beyond the loan payment

Allow separately for taxes, insurance, maintenance, utilities, lender fees and emergency savings. Test a higher interest rate and ask a lender to check the assumptions before setting a property budget.
