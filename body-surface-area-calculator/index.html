Mosteller body surface area formula

BSA in m² = √(height in cm × weight in kg ÷ 3600). At 175 cm and 70 kg the estimate is 1.84 m². This release uses the Mosteller equation consistently in all language versions.

Purpose and limitations

This is an educational estimate of body surface area, not a medication-dose calculator. Different equations can give different values. Do not calculate or change a drug dose from this result. Method: Mosteller (1987), Simplified calculation of body-surface area.
