What this simple model calculates

Illustrative litres per day = (weight in kg × 35 + active minutes × 12) ÷ 1000. For 78 kg and 45 active minutes, the result is 3.27 litres. The model is a rough arithmetic rule, not a validated individual fluid prescription.

Do not treat the result as a required amount

Actual needs depend on food, weather, sweat loss, illness and other circumstances. The activity allowance does not measure sweat loss. Some medical conditions require fluid limits; drinking excessive water can also be harmful. Follow individual clinical advice where relevant rather than forcing yourself to reach this number.
