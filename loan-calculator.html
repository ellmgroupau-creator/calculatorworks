Loan Calculator

This loan calculator helps estimate loan repayments, total interest, and general borrowing costs using common repayment assumptions.

How Loan Calculations Work

Loan repayment calculations generally depend on the amount borrowed, the interest rate, repayment frequency, and loan term.

Common Inputs

  • Loan amount
  • Interest rate
  • Loan term
  • Repayment frequency

Important Information

This calculator provides general estimates only. Lending rules, fees, repayment structures, interest calculations, and regulations vary between countries, regions, and financial institutions.
