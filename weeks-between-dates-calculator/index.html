Calculating Weeks Between Dates

Weeks-between-dates calculations help convert a date range into weeks and days. This is useful for pregnancy tracking, study schedules, project timelines, training plans, leave planning, and deadline management.

Why Weeks Are Useful for Planning

Weeks are often easier to work with than individual days for medium-term planning. They help structure recurring schedules, milestones, and progress checkpoints.

Common Uses

  • Planning projects and work schedules
  • Tracking study or training programs
  • Estimating leave or travel periods
  • Counting weeks until an important date
