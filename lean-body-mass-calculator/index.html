Separate total weight into fat and lean components

Lean body mass = body weight × (1 − body fat percentage ÷ 100). Fat mass = body weight × body fat percentage ÷ 100. For 82 kg at 19% body fat, lean mass is 66.42 kg and fat mass is 15.58 kg.

Lean mass is not just muscle

The lean component includes water, organs, bone and other non-fat tissues. The result inherits any error in the body-fat percentage you enter; hydration and measurement methods can change that estimate. This is not a body-composition measurement or a clinical assessment.
