Crawl & Index Policy

CalculatorWorks is structured to provide clear crawl pathways between calculators, category hubs, and educational resources.

Internal Linking

  • Category hubs link to calculators
  • Educational guides link to related calculators
  • Finance calculators link to supporting educational resources
  • Related calculators improve topic discovery

Sitemap Structure

Important calculator pages, educational resources, and category hubs are included in the sitemap structure to assist search engine discovery.

Content Structure

CalculatorWorks aims to reduce repetitive phrasing and improve content consistency across pages while maintaining usability and educational value.
