How Investment Growth Works

Investment growth occurs when assets increase in value over time through capital appreciation, dividends, interest, or reinvestment.

Long-Term Investing

Long-term investing may allow compounding returns to significantly increase portfolio value over decades.

Investment Diversification

Diversification is commonly used to help manage investment risk across different asset classes.

Investment Risk

All investments carry some level of risk and returns are not guaranteed.
