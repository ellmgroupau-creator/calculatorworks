Privacy Policy

CalculatorWorks privacy policy covering cookies, analytics, advertising, data handling, and user choices.

Information handled by the site

CalculatorWorks is a static calculator website. Calculator inputs are generally processed in the browser so visitors can receive quick estimates without submitting calculation values to a server-side calculator database.

Standard hosting, security, analytics, consent, and advertising technologies may process limited technical information such as device type, browser type, pages visited, referring pages, IP-derived region, and consent choices.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Cookies or similar technologies may be used for essential site functionality, consent preferences, analytics, performance measurement, security, or advertising where enabled. Visitors can manage cookies through browser settings and any consent tools shown on the site.

Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to help deliver, measure, and improve ads. The availability and behaviour of advertising features can vary by country, browser settings, and consent choices.

Contact and correction information

If a visitor contacts CalculatorWorks about a correction, suggestion, or website issue, information provided in that message may be used to review the request and respond where appropriate.

Use of CalculatorWorks is also subject to the terms of use and disclaimer.

Google advertising and cookies

When Google advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You can manage or opt out of personalised advertising through Google My Ad Center. You can also visit AboutAds choices to learn about participating third-party advertising opt-outs. Read how Google uses information from partner sites.

Consent and your choices

Where a consent message is shown, use its controls to accept, reject or manage the available purposes. You may also block or delete cookies through your browser. These controls are separate from the calculator’s Calculate and Reset buttons. Advertising availability and personalisation depend on your region, consent choices and the services enabled on the site.

Calculator inputs and contact

The calculator code in this release runs in your browser and does not send calculation values to a CalculatorWorks calculator database. Hosting and enabled third-party services may process technical information. Information you send by email is handled to review and respond to your enquiry. Contact contact@calculatorworks.io about privacy questions.
