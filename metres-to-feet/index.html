Feet, inches and metres

One international foot is exactly 0.3048 metres; one inch is exactly 0.0254 metres. Feet and inches are separate units: 5 feet 6 inches is 5.5 feet, not 5.6 feet.

Conversion formulas

metres = feet × 0.3048 + inches × 0.0254
total inches = metres ÷ 0.0254

For metres to feet and inches, divide total inches by 12, take the whole feet, and keep the remaining inches. Display rounding does not change the conversion factors.

Worked examples

5 feet 6 inches = 1.6764 metres. Conversely, 1.8 metres is approximately 5 feet 10.87 inches. Rounded inches that reach 12 are carried into the next foot.

Understanding Metres to Feet Conversions

Metres to feet conversions are frequently used in architecture, sports, aviation, travel, property listings, and everyday measurement comparisons between metric and imperial systems.

Metric vs Imperial Measurements

Most countries primarily use the metric system, while some industries and regions still commonly use feet and inches. Conversion tools help standardize measurements across both systems.

Practical Conversion Scenarios

Users commonly convert measurements for height, room dimensions, property sizing, construction planning, and international product specifications.
