Calculate gravel volume in cubic metres

Volume = area in square metres × depth in metres. This page’s depth input uses metres: 10 cm is 0.10 m. For 42 m² at 0.08 m depth, volume = 3.36 m³.

Volume is different from weight

This result is volume, not tonnes. To estimate weight, multiply cubic metres by the supplier’s bulk density in tonnes per cubic metre. Compaction, moisture, uneven ground and delivery allowances can change the order quantity. Confirm with the supplier before ordering.
