A consistent 4-point grading scale

This tool accepts grade points from 0 to 4 or letters: A+/A = 4, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2, C− = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1, D− = 0.7 and F = 0. Your institution may use a different scale.

Weighted and unweighted GPA

Weighted GPA = sum of grade points × course credits, divided by total credits. Enter one positive credit value for each grade. Leave credits blank for equal weighting. A, B+, A− with credits 3, 3, 4 gives (12 + 9.9 + 14.8) ÷ 10 = 3.67. This is course-credit weighting, not an honours-course bonus scale.

Before using this for an application

Check your institution’s rounding, repeated-course and pass/fail rules. This calculator does not decide which courses count towards your official GPA.

Understanding GPA Calculations

Grade Point Average (GPA) calculations are commonly used by schools, universities, scholarship programs, and employers to evaluate academic performance over time.

Why GPA Matters

Many academic programs use GPA benchmarks for admissions, scholarships, and graduation requirements. Tracking GPA regularly can help students better understand academic progress.

Improving Academic Planning

Students often use GPA calculators to compare grade scenarios, estimate future outcomes, and identify areas where improved performance could affect overall averages.
