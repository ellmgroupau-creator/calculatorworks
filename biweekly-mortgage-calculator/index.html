Two different ways to pay every two weeks

A budget-equivalent payment is the monthly payment × 12 ÷ 26. It spreads the same annual amount over 26 payments. An accelerated payment is half the monthly payment every two weeks: 26 half-payments equal 13 monthly payments per year.

How the comparison works

The standard monthly payment uses the fixed-rate amortisation formula. Both two-week amounts are shown alongside their annual totals. The accelerated option pays one extra monthly payment each year; this calculator does not predict its exact payoff date or interest saving.

Worked example

At $500,000, 6% and 30 years, the monthly payment is about $2,997.75. The budget-equivalent two-week amount is $1,383.58, or $35,973.03 per year before rounding. The accelerated amount is $1,498.88, or $38,970.78 per year before rounding.

Check how your lender applies payments

Some services hold partial payments until a full monthly payment is available. Fees, daily interest and processing dates can affect savings. Every two weeks means 26 payments in the model; twice monthly means 24 and is different. Use the extra-payment tool for a monthly overpayment scenario.
