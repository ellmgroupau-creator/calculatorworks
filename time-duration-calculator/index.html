How it works

If the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator assumes the end time is the following day and adds 24 hours.

How to calculate time duration correctly

A time duration calculator measures the amount of time between two times or dates. It is useful for work hours, travel planning, study sessions, project tracking, billing, and scheduling. The most important detail is whether the end time occurs on the same day, the next day, or across multiple dates.

Where duration calculations are useful

  • Timesheets, payroll, and shift length checks
  • Project planning and billable hours
  • Travel time and itinerary planning
  • Event timing, countdowns, and schedule gaps
  • Study, training, and productivity tracking

Example

If a shift starts at 9:15 AM and ends at 5:45 PM, the duration is 8 hours and 30 minutes. If a shift starts at 10:00 PM and ends at 6:00 AM, the calculation crosses midnight and should be treated as an overnight duration.

Common mistakes

Watch for AM/PM errors, time zones, daylight saving changes, unpaid breaks, and overnight periods. These details can make a large difference in payroll, travel, and scheduling contexts.

Understanding Time Duration Calculations

Time duration calculators help measure the amount of time between two points. They are useful for work shifts, project tracking, travel planning, event timing, study schedules, and productivity analysis.

Why Duration Tracking Helps

Accurate duration calculations can improve payroll estimates, scheduling, billing, and deadline planning. They also reduce mistakes when working across hours, minutes, days, or overnight time ranges.

Common Uses

  • Calculating work shift length
  • Tracking project or task time
  • Planning travel and event schedules
  • Estimating billable hours or study time
