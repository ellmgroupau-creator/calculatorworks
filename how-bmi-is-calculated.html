How BMI Is Calculated

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a widely used screening measurement that estimates whether body weight falls within standard healthy ranges relative to height.

BMI Formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m²)

Example BMI Calculation

If a person weighs 80 kg and has a height of 1.75 m:

  • 1.75 × 1.75 = 3.06
  • 80 ÷ 3.06 = 26.1 BMI

Standard BMI Categories

  • Underweight
  • Healthy weight
  • Overweight
  • Obesity ranges

Limitations of BMI

BMI does not directly measure body composition, muscle mass, or overall health status.

Healthcare professionals may use additional measurements and medical assessments alongside BMI.
