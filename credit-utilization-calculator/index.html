Formula and example

Credit utilisation = statement or current balance ÷ credit limit × 100. A $4,200 balance on an $18,000 limit gives 23.33%. Use balances and limits from the same point in time.

One card or your combined accounts?

For combined utilisation, add the balances first and divide by the combined credit limits. Do not average card percentages: a small-limit card and a large-limit card carry different weights. A result above 100% indicates the balance exceeds the entered limit.

What this cannot predict

The percentage is not a credit score forecast. Reporting dates, individual-card balances and scoring models can affect how lenders interpret utilisation. You do not need to pay interest just to calculate or track this ratio.
