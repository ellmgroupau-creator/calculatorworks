Editorial Policy

CalculatorWorks aims to publish clear, practical, and reliable calculator tools supported by educational explanations.

Content principles

  • Use plain language wherever possible.
  • Explain formulas and assumptions clearly.
  • Keep sensitive topics general, educational, and appropriately caveated.
  • Avoid presenting calculator outputs as professional advice.
  • Improve pages over time as the calculator library expands.

Accuracy approach

CalculatorWorks uses standard formulas where appropriate and works to present calculations in a transparent way. However, calculators may simplify complex real-world situations.

Financial, health, and specialist topics

Pages involving finance, tax, health, construction, or similar subjects are written as general educational material. They are not intended to replace qualified professional advice.

Updates

CalculatorWorks pages may be updated to improve clarity, usability, internal linking, examples, and explanatory depth.

CalculatorWorks publishes free calculator tools and educational resources. Results and content are for general guidance only and should be checked before important financial, legal, medical, tax, construction, or health decisions.
