How to use paint results

Measure wall dimensions carefully and subtract large openings such as doors or windows if the calculator allows it. Coverage depends on surface texture, paint type, colour change, and the number of coats.

Before relying on the result: Always check the coverage rate on the specific paint product and consider primer, wastage, and touch-ups.
