Feet, inches and metres

One international foot is exactly 0.3048 metres; one inch is exactly 0.0254 metres. Feet and inches are separate units: 5 feet 6 inches is 5.5 feet, not 5.6 feet.

Conversion formulas

metres = feet × 0.3048 + inches × 0.0254
total inches = metres ÷ 0.0254

For metres to feet and inches, divide total inches by 12, take the whole feet, and keep the remaining inches. Display rounding does not change the conversion factors.

Worked examples

5 feet 6 inches = 1.6764 metres. Conversely, 1.8 metres is approximately 5 feet 10.87 inches. Rounded inches that reach 12 are carried into the next foot.

When to Use a Feet to Metres Conversion

Feet to metres conversions are commonly used for height measurements, property dimensions, construction planning, engineering specifications, sports measurements, and international measurement comparisons.

Why Accurate Conversion Matters

Even small conversion differences can affect construction plans, room sizing, product specifications, and measurement reporting. Accurate metric conversion is particularly important when working across countries that use different measurement systems.

Common Real-World Examples

  • Converting personal height measurements
  • Comparing building dimensions
  • Understanding international specifications
  • Travel and property measurement comparisons
