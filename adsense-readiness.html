Site Quality and Advertising Information

CalculatorWorks is built as a user-first calculator and educational resource platform.

Advertising approach

If advertising is displayed, the goal is to keep calculator functionality, navigation, and educational content easy to access.

User experience

Ads should not be designed to look like calculator buttons, navigation links, form inputs, or site controls.

Content-first structure

CalculatorWorks prioritizes useful tools, clear explanations, related calculators, and educational resources before monetization.

CalculatorWorks publishes free calculator tools and educational resources. Results and content are for general guidance only and should be checked before important financial, legal, medical, tax, construction, or health decisions.
